01 / A LITTLE ABOUT US

More than a club.
A mindset.

Shrishti is about turning environmental awareness into everyday action.

Eco Club Shrishti brings students together to learn about the world around us, explore practical solutions, and encourage our school community to make more sustainable choices. From reducing waste to caring for nature, meaningful change begins with people willing to try.
